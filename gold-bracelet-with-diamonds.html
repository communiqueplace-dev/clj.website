Gold Bracelet with Diamonds

A slim gold bracelet brightened with a scattering of bezel-set diamonds, this piece is built for daily wear rather than special occasions. Bezel setting surrounds each diamond in a thin rim of metal rather than exposed prongs, which protects the stone from daily knocks and makes the bracelet more practical to wear without much thought. The diamonds are spaced rather than clustered, so the gold itself still reads as the main material. A comfortable, low-maintenance piece for anyone who wants a little sparkle without a statement bracelet's upkeep. Gold Jewellery by C.L Khanna Jewellers, Lawrence Road Amritsar. BIS hallmarked, 18k Gold.
